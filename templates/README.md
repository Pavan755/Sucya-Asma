# Templates

Copy the relevant template into an episode/character folder and fill in its IDs and version. Keep the template generic; project-specific claims, sources, prompts, and approvals belong in the copied production artifact.

- `story-template.md` — episode brief and production record.
- `character-bible-template.md` — character identity, sources, references and version history.
- `environment-template.md` — location, period details, sources and continuity.
- `performance-template.md` — voice and acting guidance.
- `motion-template.md` — movement vocabulary and constraints.
- `prompt-template.md` — generation inputs and outputs with provenance.
- `approval-template.md` — version-specific human decision.
