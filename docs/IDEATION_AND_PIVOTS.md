# Ideation and pivots

## Idea card

```text
Idea ID / version:
Working title:
One-sentence premise:
Audience:
Central question:
Viewer takeaway:
Why this could matter:
Possible primary sources:
Story/source category:
Character opportunities:
Visual opportunities:
Cultural, ethical or rights questions:
Runtime/format:
Effort estimate:
Unknowns:
```

Compare ideas on source strength, question clarity, audience relevance, visual potential, feasibility, and respectful treatment. Rate each 1–5 with a reason. Scores inform discussion; unsupported claims cannot be offset by visual appeal.

## Pivot triggers

- Strong evidence contradicts the premise.
- Essential sources are inaccessible or cannot be used lawfully.
- The story cannot fit the intended format.
- A character-led angle distorts evidence or tradition.
- Rights or cultural concerns cannot be resolved.
- Research reveals a clearer question.

## Pivot record

```text
Date:
Old idea/artifact version:
Trigger and evidence:
What no longer works:
New question/thesis:
What carries forward:
What must be rechecked or discarded:
Impact on script, characters, assets, runtime, rights and effort:
Decision/reason:
Approver:
```

Archive old versions. Reuse assets only after checking context, rights and implied claim. Re-run claim checks for affected lines and shots.
